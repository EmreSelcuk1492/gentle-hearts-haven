The approach

Gentle movement

Questions

What you might be wondering

Many people arrive with questions — and with healthy skepticism. That's welcome here.

Do I need to believe in energy healing for this to work?

Not at all. Many clients come as skeptics — including engineers, doctors, and corporate professionals — and find real, lasting change. The practices (breathwork, movement, meditation) are grounded in how the nervous system and body actually work. You just need to be willing to try.

What does a session actually look like?

Sessions are guided and completely personalized. Asli begins by understanding what you're carrying — stress, a health challenge, an emotional pattern — then guides you through specific breathing techniques, gentle movement, and meditation to help your body and mind begin releasing it. Sessions can be in-person or remote.

How is this different from therapy or coaching?

Therapy primarily works with thoughts and words. This work addresses the body and energy system directly — where emotions, trauma, and chronic stress are often stored. Many clients use this work alongside therapy or medical care for a more complete approach to healing.

How quickly will I notice a difference?

Many clients notice a shift — feeling lighter, calmer, or clearer — after their first session. Deeper healing of chronic patterns typically unfolds over weeks or months. Asli will co-create a plan with you based on what you're working through.

Are sessions available in Turkish?

Yes. Asli is fully bilingual in English and Turkish and welcomes clients in both languages.

Ready to begin?

Start with a free 20-minute discovery call. No pressure, no commitment — just a conversation about where you are and where you'd like to be.
